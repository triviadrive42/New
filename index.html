HOW TO PLAY

Answer trivia to drive down the field. Everyone gets the same game each day.

OFFENSE

  • Pick a play: EASY +5, MEDIUM +10, HARD +20 yards.
  • Past midfield: Medium or Hard only. Red zone (inside the 20): Hard only, and a right answer is a touchdown.
  • 4 downs to gain 10 yards. A wrong answer is an incomplete pass.
  • Miss a HARD question and there's a 1-in-4 chance it's intercepted.
  • Field goal (Medium question) from the opponent's 30 or closer. Punt on 4th down.
  • After a TD: extra point (Easy, +1) or go for two (Hard, +2).

DEFENSE

  • Each CPU drive is 3 snaps. A right answer is a stop.
  • BASE is a Medium question. BLITZ is Hard: right is a stop with a 1-in-3 takeaway chance, wrong counts as two misses.
  • More stops means the CPU punts instead of scoring.

COACH'S CHALLENGE

  • Once per game, throw the flag after a wrong answer. You get a new question at the same level. Get it right and the play is overturned.

EVERY DAY IS DIFFERENT

  • A new made-up opponent with its own style.
  • Themes: Rules Tuesday, College Wednesday, Throwback Thursday, Legends Friday, Championship Saturday. Monday mixes everything.
  • Weather: snow (10-second timer), wind (Hard field goals), rain (more interceptions).
  • Your week runs Monday to Sunday. Sunday is a playoff game against an All-Pro CPU.

CLOCK

  • 15 seconds to answer. Four 7-minute quarters.
  • Right answers burn 30 seconds plus your answer time. Wrong ones burn 5.
  • Use a timeout after a gain to stop the clock.

PRACTICE REPLAY

  • After you finish today's Daily, you can replay it as many times as you like. Replays never change your stats, streak, week or league result.

STREAKS

  • Every 7 days in a row earns a bye (hold up to 2). A bye covers a missed day so your streak survives.
